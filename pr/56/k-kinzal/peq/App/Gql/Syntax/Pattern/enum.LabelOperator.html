enum LabelOperator

How a label expression is built up.

Labels are what a pattern selects by, and GQL lets them be combined the way a boolean expression is: Method|Function for either, Member&!Property for one but not the other, % for anything at all. On a graph of PHP symbols that is more useful than it sounds — "every callable that is not a method" and "every class-like

  • that is not an interface" are both one pattern rather than two queries.
