The functions GQL offers that belong to no one kind of value.

coalesce and nullif are the two moves a query makes around absent values: the first supplies one, the second withdraws one. Between them they are how a reader escapes three-valued logic without writing a CASE.

to_json_string is the one that matters most for the reader peq is built for. An agent that has matched a pattern and wants the whole of what it found — labels, properties and all — asks for it as JSON in one column rather than enumerating properties it would have to know the names of first.
