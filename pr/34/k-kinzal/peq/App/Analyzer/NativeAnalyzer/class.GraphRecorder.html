Collects what a walk finds, and turns it into a graph once the walk is over.

A walk meets a symbol and a relation to it in whatever order the source happens to be written, and a graph cannot be built in that order: recording a relation before the symbol it points at would leave the graph holding a placeholder where it could have held the symbol itself. Everything found is therefore collected first, and the graph is built from it in two passes, symbols before relations.
