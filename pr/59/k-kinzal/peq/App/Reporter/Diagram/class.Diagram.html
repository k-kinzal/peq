The symbols and relations a drawing is made of.

A dependency graph is not a tree, and drawing it as one loses the two things that make it a graph: a symbol several branches reach is one symbol, and a cycle is a relation rather than a word at the end of a branch. A drawing numbers every symbol once and shows every relation between the symbols it holds, so both come back.

Only relations between symbols the drawing holds are kept. A relation pointing out of what was drawn would be an arrow to nowhere, which says less than leaving it out: the reader would not be able to tell a symbol that was left out from one that does not exist.
