final class DiagramNode

One symbol as a drawing shows it.

Deliberately not a graph node. Two very different things are drawn as diagrams — the walk an inspection made, and the elements a query found — and the drawing should not have to know which. What it needs is what a reader needs: what the symbol is called, what kind of thing it is, and where to go and look at it.
