enum UnaryOperator

An operator written before the one value it acts on.

The null tests and the truth-value tests are here rather than among the comparisons because that is what they are: IS NULL and IS UNKNOWN ask about one value and cannot be undecided, which is exactly what makes them the way out of three-valued logic. Writing them as comparisons would make them inherit the undecidability they exist to escape.
