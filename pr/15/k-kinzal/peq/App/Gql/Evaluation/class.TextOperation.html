Writing one value after another, which is the only operator GQL spells with strings.

|| joins two character strings into one and two lists into one, and that is all it does: ISO/IEC 39075 writes <character string concatenation> and <list concatenation> and nothing that joins a string to a number. 'line ' || 12 is therefore a value of the wrong type rather than 'line 12'.

The three predicates a reader coming from another graph language reaches for first — CONTAINS, STARTS WITH, ENDS WITH — are not here either, because the standard does not define them. A prefix is asked for with left, a suffix with right.
