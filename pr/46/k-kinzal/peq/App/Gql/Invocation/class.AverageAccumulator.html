What they come to on average.

The average of exact numbers is exact: ISO/IEC 39075 makes its declared type an exact numeric one and leaves which to the implementation (ID096). peq's is a decimal with the larger of the inputs' scales and at least six digits after the point, cut off rather than rounded — so the average of 1 and 2 is 1.500000, not a float. An approximate value among them makes the average approximate.
