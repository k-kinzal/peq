One step along a path, as a tree shows it.

A path a query bound is a chain of relations and symbols, and a tree of paths is those chains with their common beginnings drawn once. A step is therefore the pair that makes a chain comparable: how the reader got here, and where here is.

Returns what tells this step apart from any other.

Two paths share a beginning when they took the same relations to the same symbols, the same way round, which means every part of a step counts: a class reached by being extended and by being called is reached two different ways, and so is a method reached by calling it and by being called by it.
